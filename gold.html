    FAQ

    Frequently asked questions.

    Do I need an appointment to sell gold?+

    No. Walk in any time during opening hours. For large amounts or bullion, a quick WhatsApp message beforehand helps us have the right cash ready.

    What do I need to bring?+

    The gold and a valid photo ID. Receipts and certificates are helpful but not required.

    Does testing damage my jewellery?+

    No. XRF analysis reads the metal's composition without scratching, filing or using acid.

    Why is your offer lower than the spot price?+

    Spot is the price of pure gold on the international market. Scrap is refined before it can be resold, so the offer reflects the gold's purity and refining costs. We show you the figures behind it.

    Will you buy back a bar I bought elsewhere?+

    Yes. Bars from recognised refiners are bought at the live rate after verification.
